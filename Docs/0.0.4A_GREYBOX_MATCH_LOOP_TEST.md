## Baseline Rules Implemented

- Mode: REBUT KURSI
- Team assignment: alternating client IDs, CYAN for even IDs and ORANGE for odd IDs
- 4v4-ready spawn slots: P0–P7
- Match timer: 7:00
- Countdown: 5 seconds
- Kursi capture time: 3 seconds
- Both teams in the capture zone: DIPEREBUTKAN; capture pauses
- Captured Kursi does not generate POWER by itself
- A member of the owning team must approach the Kursi and press DUDUK
- Seated player becomes PENGUASA
- PENGUASA cannot use Basic Attack
- PENGUASA cannot move normally
- PENGUASA may TURUN or use DODGE to leave the Kursi
- Active PENGUASA generates +1 POWER per second
- First team to 100 POWER wins
- WIBAWA RUNTUH clears PENGUASA and stops POWER generation
- WIBAWA respawn baseline: 6 seconds
- DODGE cooldown baseline: 4 seconds
- Friendly fire disabled
- On normal timer expiry: higher POWER wins
- Timer expiry while capture is DIPEREBUTKAN: MASA KRITIS / overtime
- Tied POWER after the timer: SUDDEN POWER
- SUDDEN POWER: first team to earn 5 new POWER wins
- RESULT state locks normal gameplay
- Host can REMATCH and reset both teams / players

## Host-only Greybox Test Controls

These are temporary validation controls and are not production gameplay:

- TEST TIMER 10s — reduces the current PLAYING timer to 10 seconds
- TEST POWER 95 — raises both teams to at least 95 POWER

They exist to validate timeout, overtime, sudden-power, and win conditions without consuming a seven-minute test for every case.

## One-Build Device Test

1. Install the exact same APK on all participating Android devices.
2. Device A presses HOST.
3. Other devices enter Host LAN IPv4 and press CLIENT.
4. Confirm player team assignment:
   - P0 CYAN
   - P1 ORANGE
   - P2 CYAN
   - P3 ORANGE
   - continues alternating through P7.
5. Host presses START MATCH.
6. Confirm 5-second COUNTDOWN and movement/attack lock before PLAYING.
7. Confirm players spawn/reset at team-side spawn markers.
8. Enter the Kursi zone with one team only.
9. Confirm CAPTURE progress reaches 100% in about 3 seconds.
10. Confirm entering the zone with both teams changes status to DIPEREBUTKAN and capture stops.
11. After capture, confirm POWER does not increase until a player presses DUDUK.
12. Confirm DUDUK only enables for a player from the team that owns the Kursi and is close enough.
13. Confirm PENGUASA is pinned to the Kursi.
14. Confirm PENGUASA cannot ATTACK.
15. Confirm POWER increases +1 per second while PENGUASA remains seated.
16. Confirm TURUN stops POWER generation.
17. Confirm DODGE lets PENGUASA leave the Kursi and stops POWER generation.
18. Confirm enemy team can recapture after the Kursi is available.
19. Confirm same-team Basic Attack does not damage a teammate.
20. Confirm enemy Basic Attack reduces WIBAWA.
21. Confirm WIBAWA 0 shows WIBAWA RUNTUH.
22. Confirm a defeated PENGUASA immediately stops POWER generation.
23. Confirm respawn occurs after about 6 seconds at the correct team spawn.
24. Use TEST POWER 95, seat a PENGUASA, and confirm first team to 100 reaches RESULT.
25. Confirm RESULT locks movement/attack/dodge.
26. Confirm winning team is shown.
27. Press REMATCH and confirm POWER, objective, WIBAWA, and spawn positions reset.
28. Start another match and use TEST TIMER 10s.
29. Let time expire with unequal POWER; confirm higher POWER team wins.
30. Rematch, use TEST TIMER 10s, and contest the Kursi at zero; confirm MASA KRITIS.
31. Break the contest; confirm overtime resolves.
32. Rematch, keep POWER tied, use TEST TIMER 10s, let the timer expire without a contest; confirm SUDDEN POWER.
33. In SUDDEN POWER, seat a ruler and confirm first team to +5 new POWER wins.
34. Confirm Chair visual changes with neutral/capture/team/contested/Sudden Power state.
35. Confirm STOP still returns the networking proof to a restartable state without crashing.

## PASS Gate

0.0.4A is PASS when the match can proceed end-to-end:

WAITING -> COUNTDOWN -> PLAYING -> objective capture -> PENGUASA -> POWER -> WIBAWA RUNTUH / respawn -> RESULT -> REMATCH

and the timeout branches MASA KRITIS and SUDDEN POWER are also proven on at least one two-device test.
