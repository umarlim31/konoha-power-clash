## Source

Branch:

`feature/v0.0.6a2-hero-greybox-polish`

Expected Android footer:

`0.0.6A.2 • Hero Identity + Greybox Polish`

Expected Android app version:

- Version: `0.0.6.2`
- versionCode: `12`

## Goal

This sub-phase does not redesign combat, networking, bots, objective rules, or match flow.

Its job is to make the existing 4v4 prototype stop reading like:

- colored capsules,
- random cubes,
- flat empty floor,
- debug arena.

The target is still intentionally low-cost greybox/prototype art, but every visible shape should now have a reason.

---

# Hero Identity Pass

The four hero visuals remain original prototype silhouettes rather than likeness copies.

## MEGA

Visual language:

- broad silhouette,
- dark helmet shell,
- rounded shoulder guards,
- light horn accents,
- red chest plate.

The goal is to read as a heavy frontliner at gameplay distance without relying only on the nameplate.

## PRABOWO

Visual language:

- large command silhouette,
- dark helmet shell,
- metallic shoulder plates,
- gold chest plate,
- small command crest.

The goal is a sturdy commander/bruiser read distinct from MEGA.

## ABAH

Visual language:

- lighter body silhouette,
- compact head/cap shell,
- asymmetric blue scarf pieces,
- light book-like side prop,
- small blue signal accent.

The goal is a ranged controller/readability silhouette rather than another armored fighter.

## JOKOWI

Visual language:

- compact utility silhouette,
- dark head shell,
- green front vest,
- metallic utility pack,
- light tool handle,
- green tool head.

The goal is an infrastructure/utility archetype without making the prop oversized.

---

# Actor Readability

## Nameplates

Actor labels are shortened.

Examples:

- `MEGA • YOU`
- `PRABOWO • P1`
- `ABAH • BOT`

A PENGUASA receives a second line:

`PENGUASA`

This reduces text clutter in an 8-actor fight.

## Facing marker

The old protruding rectangular facing block is reduced to a smaller directional cue so it no longer reads like a random arm/weapon.

## Team and local identity

- Team affiliation remains communicated by the team-colored body/ring.
- Local-player ground marker remains near-white.
- YOU remains highlighted in warm gold.
- RUNTUH still dims the actor and hero accessories.

---

# Arena Greybox Pass

## Floor hierarchy

The arena is now separated into clear spatial layers:

- dark primary floor,
- center combat lane,
- north/south side lanes,
- CYAN spawn bay,
- ORANGE spawn bay.

The purpose is to make the map readable even before final environment art.

## Team base language

Both teams receive mirrored visual language:

### CYAN
- cyan spawn bay,
- cyan directional chevrons,
- cyan wall stripe,
- cyan spawn discs.

### ORANGE
- orange spawn bay,
- orange directional chevrons,
- orange wall stripe,
- orange spawn discs.

No party/organization logos are used.

## Cover language

The two major center covers are no longer plain boxes.

Each is constructed as:

- gameplay collision body,
- capped top plate,
- side accent strip,
- small slanted detail.

Their gameplay footprint remains approximately equivalent to the prior cover.

## Arena landmarks

The arena now includes:

- relay blocks,
- relay caps,
- side landmarks,
- structural wall trims.

These are intended to help spatial orientation before camera-control work starts.

## Objective hierarchy

The Kursi area now uses:

- lower dark dais,
- upper gold dais,
- muted capture zone,
- four objective tick marks,
- slightly refined chair proportions,
- a crown/header detail.

The physical Kursi remains visually dominant while the capture zone stays subdued.

## Spawn presentation

Square spawn tiles are replaced by:

- thin circular spawn discs,
- directional floor chevrons.

This makes the start formation look intentional instead of like debug pads.

---

# Lighting Pass

The map uses a simple mobile-friendly two-directional-light setup:

- brighter primary light,
- low-intensity cool fill,
- darker ambient base.

No realtime shadows are introduced.

The goal is more readable form separation without adding a significant Android rendering burden.

---

# Non-Goals

0.0.6A.2 intentionally does not implement:

- free camera control,
- pre-match cinematic camera,
- pinch zoom,
- camera rotation,
- final character models,
- final textures,
- final environment assets,
- animation system,
- new gameplay abilities,
- new balance changes.

Those camera/presentation systems belong to the planned 0.0.6B phase after this visual foundation passes.
