# 0.0.6A.1 — Video Review Fix Pack

Source reviewed: first 0.0.6A Android gameplay recording.

## What worked in the recording

- Full 4v4 match was running.
- Center scoreboard was present.
- Team Power presentation was readable.
- Objective/capture presentation worked.
- Four prototype hero identities were visible.
- World WIBAWA bars were present.
- RUNTUH feedback and replicated KO feed worked.
- Local hero ability controls remained functional.
- Match networking/status stayed connected during the recording.

## Problems observed

### 1. Persistent ability VFX covered too much of the arena

ABAH Narasi used a large opaque blue floor rectangle.

JOKOWI Infrastruktur used a large opaque green road rectangle.

With bots using abilities automatically, multiple persistent zones could cover a large percentage of the arena and hide:

- arena landmarks,
- team bays,
- character silhouettes,
- spawn/readability cues,
- objective state.

### Fix

Persistent area VFX are now sparse guides instead of filled slabs.

- Narasi = blue perimeter outline + small signal marks.
- Infrastruktur = two lane edges + dashed center line.
- Short-lived generic skill VFX are smaller and darker.

Gameplay ranges/durations are not changed by this visual correction.

---

### 2. Hero accessories remained too bright during RUNTUH

The body correctly darkened on knockout, but prototype hero accessories could remain fully saturated.

### Fix

Hero accessories and team rings now react to RUNTUH and darken together with the actor.

---

### 3. Prototype hero props were oversized

Several silhouette props projected too far from the capsule and could read as random blocks rather than identity cues.

### Fix

All four greybox identity kits were compacted.

- MEGA shoulders/horns reduced.
- PRABOWO armor/shoulders reduced.
- ABAH scarf/book/signal reduced.
- JOKOWI tool/beam/vest reduced.

The hero remains distinguishable but the silhouette no longer dominates nearby actors.

---

### 4. Local player marker conflicted with gameplay colors

The previous green local-player marker could be confused with JOKOWI/green skill VFX.

### Fix

- Local ground ring is now team-neutral near-white.
- Local nameplate uses a warm gold highlight for YOU.
- Team affiliation remains communicated by actor body/team ring.

---

### 5. Objective disk was visually too strong

The Kursi zone could become a large saturated CYAN/ORANGE disk.

### Fix

The objective floor zone is now substantially darker while the physical chair stays bright enough to communicate ownership.

The top objective banner/capture bar remains the primary status presentation.

---

### 6. Square joystick still looked like debug UI

The large square movement pad was one of the strongest remaining prototype cues.

### Fix

The joystick is now rendered procedurally as:

- circular dark movement pad,
- circular cyan handle,
- slightly smaller footprint.

No joystick/input behavior is changed.

---

### 7. KO feed background had too much empty space

A single knockout could display inside a large fixed black panel.

### Fix

The RUNTUH feed now auto-sizes to the number of active feed entries and keeps a maximum of three visible events.

---

### 8. Scoreboard center rows overlapped slightly

The tiny roster line and objective banner could visually collide.

### Fix

Scoreboard height/spacing was adjusted so timer, state, roster, objective banner and capture line each have their own vertical space.

---

### 9. Build footer was too prominent

The build label contributed to the debug/prototype feel.

### Fix

The footer is now smaller, dimmer and uses the new patch identifier.
