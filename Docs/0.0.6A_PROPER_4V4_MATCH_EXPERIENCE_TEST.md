## Goal

0.0.6A is a presentation/readability milestone. It keeps the proven 4v4 Rebut Kursi gameplay, hero kits, networking, and bot AI from 0.0.5C while reducing the greybox feel and making a full eight-actor fight easier to read on a phone.

No core win-condition redesign is intended in this batch.

## Implemented Experience Layer

### 1. Mirrored 4v4 spawn formation

Each team now starts in a mirrored formation rather than a straight line.

- CYAN starts on the left side and faces the objective.
- ORANGE starts on the right side and faces the objective.
- Four team slots are spread across front/back rows.
- Respawns reuse the same formation and facing.
- Bots and human players use the same deterministic formation system.

### 2. Hero identity silhouettes

All four prototype heroes retain the team-colored body while receiving a distinct greybox silhouette/accent:

- MEGA: red accent, broad shoulders, horn-like silhouette.
- PRABOWO: gold accent, larger shoulders/chest plate.
- ABAH: blue accent, lighter body silhouette, scarf/book/signal shapes.
- JOKOWI: green accent, vest/tool/beam shapes.

The local ability HUD also inherits the selected hero accent so the currently controlled hero is readable without relying only on text.

### 3. Team and local-player readability

- Actors get a team-colored ground ring.
- The local human retains a stronger ground marker.
- Nameplates stay compact and prioritize nearby actors, the local player, RUNTUH actors, and the PENGUASA.
- Hero identity remains visible in the actor label.

### 4. World-space WIBAWA bars

The old floating numeric-only WIBAWA presentation is replaced in generated player/bot prefabs by a compact world-space bar.

The bar shows:

- remaining WIBAWA as a horizontal fill,
- green / amber / red health states,
- blue shield overlay,
- a team-color strip,
- current WIBAWA value,
- `RUNTUH` state.

Bars use distance-based visibility/scaling to keep an eight-actor fight readable.

### 5. Combat feedback

Existing hit flash and floating damage feedback remain enabled.

Damage feedback supports:

- red damage values,
- blue BLOCK feedback,
- damage + shield absorption,
- brief upward floating motion.

### 6. Networked RUNTUH feed

A replicated combat feed now reports actor knockouts to every connected device.

Examples:

- `P0 MEGA > BOT ABAH RUNTUH`
- `BOT PRABOWO > P1 JOKOWI RUNTUH`

Human Basic, bot Basic, and hero-skill damage carry actor attribution into the feed.

### 7. Proper 4v4 scoreboard

The match HUD is promoted from a debug-style text block to a centered match scoreboard.

It includes:

- CYAN Power score,
- ORANGE Power score,
- team-colored Power progress bars,
- large center timer,
- match state,
- human/bot roster count,
- objective status banner,
- capture progress strip,
- contested state feedback.

### 8. Cleaner developer controls

- START / REMATCH only appears to the Host in appropriate states.
- Test controls are hidden by default.
- Host can open them using a small `DEV` control.
- TIMER 10s, POWER 95, and ULT 100 remain available for rapid regression testing.

### 9. Reduced network-debug chrome

After HOST/CLIENT is active, the network panel compresses to a small status + STOP presentation.

The pre-match connection controls are still available before the connection starts.

### 10. Arena visual pass

The arena is still intentionally low-cost greybox geometry, but now has clearer structure:

- darker arena floor,
- central lane treatment,
- CYAN and ORANGE spawn bays,
- side team walls,
- capped cover blocks,
- corner landmark pillars,
- smaller spawn pads,
- Kursi pedestal,
- stronger objective hierarchy.

This is not the final art map. Its purpose is to make gameplay composition/readability closer to a real match before final characters and environment assets arrive.

## PASS Gate

0.0.6A is PASS when:

- an eight-actor battle is materially easier to read than 0.0.5C,
- each of the four heroes has a distinguishable prototype silhouette,
- WIBAWA/shield/RUNTUH states are readable on a phone,
- combat hits and knockouts have clear feedback,
- the match score/objective can be understood without reading a large debug paragraph,
- and no networking, hero-kit, bot-AI, objective, result, or rematch regression is introduced.
