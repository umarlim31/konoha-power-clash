## Abah polish

- Word Wave BASIC: 17 damage, long range
- Word Wave hit gets visible blue feedback
- Narasi: keeps ally-speed / enemy-slow zone

The goal is not to turn Abah into a burst-damage hero. His threat should come from ranged poke, zone pressure, tempo control and Silence.

## 4v4 auto-fill

Each team targets four actors.

Human players take their normal deterministic team slot. While WAITING or RESULT:

- CYAN is filled to 4 actors
- ORANGE is filled to 4 actors
- Empty positions are filled with network bots
- If a human joins a slot occupied by a bot, the conflicting bot is removed
- The roster returns to exactly 4v4 before the next match

The HUD shows:

`4v4 8/8 | HUMANS X | BOTS Y`

A single Host can therefore start a prototype match with seven bots. Two humans normally produce six bots.

## Tactical bot v1

Bots are server-authoritative and synchronized to all clients.

They can:

- move toward the Kursi objective
- capture and contest the Kursi
- become PENGUASA when their team owns the Kursi
- generate POWER as PENGUASA
- defend around a friendly PENGUASA
- prioritize an enemy PENGUASA
- select nearby enemy combat targets
- use hero-specific BASIC range, damage and cooldown
- use S1
- use S2
- build Pengaruh
- use Ultimate at 100 Pengaruh
- use a defensive dodge when low on WIBAWA
- obey Stun
- receive Silence / knockback / shields / damage
- become WIBAWA RUNTUH
- respawn at their correct team slot
- reset correctly on REMATCH

Bot hero assignment rotates by team slot:

- slot 0: Mega
- slot 1: Prabowo
- slot 2: Abah
- slot 3: Jokowi

This is tactical AI v1, not final production AI. It deliberately prioritizes proving 4v4 readability, networking load and objective behavior.
